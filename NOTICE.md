No project-wide license has been selected for this repository. Publication does not add a license grant for the project or its dependencies.

Project Zomboid, Viewpoint and ZombieBuddy are separate dependencies. Their code, assets and names remain subject to their respective owners' terms. Their runtime JARs, extracted shader sources, native libraries and game artwork are not included as standalone dependencies here. The JARs under `Contents/` contain this addon's implementation; compilation stubs are declarations only and are excluded from runtime artifacts.

`NativeShadowMasks.java` contains compressed coordinate masks derived from shipped native artwork. `NativeSpriteShadows.java` contains partial-alpha shadow coordinates derived from shipped PNGs. These are derived coordinate data, rather than full color sprite images. This notice records their provenance and does not assert ownership of the source artwork.
